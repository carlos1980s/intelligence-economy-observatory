STRUCTURAL RANGE

—

Eight corners: demand A, productivity B and capacity K ±20%. Not a statistical confidence interval.
Show the equations and dimensional assumptions

q_d(p) = A p^(−ε)

q_s(p) = min[K, B (p/c)^η]

c = (R / 100)^ρ

pᵤ = (A/B)^(1/(ε+η)) c^(η/(ε+η)); qᵤ = A pᵤ^(−ε)

If qᵤ ≤ K, the equilibrium is (qᵤ, pᵤ). Otherwise q*=K and p*=(A/K)^(1/ε). Marginal cost at capacity is c(K/B)^(1/η); any excess market price is a conditional scarcity rent.

q and p are dimensionless ratios, shown as indices multiplied by 100. The reference A=B=c=1 with K≥1 gives q=p=1. This is a normalization, not empirical calibration. I means the annual fixed-quality task basket; its absolute worldwide scale is not identified.

The reduced-form demand scale A stands in for substitution, intensification, new activities and adoption frictions. Integration, oversight, human labor and risk require separate empirical measurement; this compact scenario does not estimate them.

R is the fixed AWS basket input-price index, not a useful-task price. ρ is assumed pass-through; c is not measured marginal cost. B summarizes assumed efficiency and utilization. K is assumed deliverable capacity. API output prices are displayed separately, never added as an independent GPU input cost.

04 / RESEARCH DESIGN

Evidence before
certainty.

The completed study finds that offered prices, task performance, adoption and economic output are distinct. The site therefore separates observed indicators from conditional equilibrium experiments.

Read the full research study →
01

A fixed-quality unit

Define and benchmark successful cognitive task baskets. Hold acceptance, latency and delivery standards constant.

02

Separate demand mechanisms

Substitution, greater intensity and newly viable activities can all move demand. Integration, oversight and risk limit adoption.

03

Observe the information set

Dates, exact model IDs, host bundles and service terms matter. NVIDIA, Broadcom and neocloud filings are indirect challenger signals.

04

Keep breaks visible

Census BTOS changed its AI question in November 2025. Before/after adoption series must not be silently spliced.
